# INTRODUCTION

## NEW PROCESS: LAST REFORM TO ENSURE EFFICIENCY

Due of the failure of the second delivery, it was decided to, not improve, but change again the process attempting to not deliver anything late again and not to miss the asigned time for an artifact, we focused on efficiency, and if posible, efficacy.

*For our new process: we applied two of the Software agile process manifesto:*

- **Working software** over comprehensive documentation
- **Responding to change** over following a plan

At least in our process, it means that the activities are started to be worked once the third deliver started, even if there is not Github project issues for it, no branches usage, no pull requests, no fixing documents, no anything, nothing, just keep with the developing of the artifacts as fast as posible before the end of a sprint, and keeping a small redaction of what was done.

The members must have come by their own to ask for activities, no calls due latement and urgencies could happen if the activities are on no one, instead the artifacts would just keep progressing.

This is because the study of the results of the past first and second deliver, this was the decision, we made changes instead of following the subject standard, because it didn't work for us.

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

### THE PROBLEM

Most of the people that starts to learn Git can't learn quickly and easy how to use it correctly from the content available in internet, this is because the text blocks are difficult to understand, and they can't see clear examples of usage and they do not finish comprehending the action.

### SOLUTION PROPOSAL

An environment where all the posible information and guides about Git is deposited, builded for unexperienced people, where the content is presented with a lot more tools like graphs and playable audios than just text. Not only that, the people could verify if they have learn it through evaluations presented with different styles and tools.

### OBJECTIVE

This is meant to help people to comprehend git and to improve their tech-skills in this tool.
