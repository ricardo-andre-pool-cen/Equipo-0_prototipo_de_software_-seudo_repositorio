# CONSTRUCTIVE CRITIC ABOUT SOFTWARE ENGINEERING FUNDAMENTALS SUBJECT

*We have reached at the end of the subject, this has been quite an experience and we have our observations about the subject and it's programm. we are dividing our thoughts in a list where we think there is an improvement area for the subject.*

- ## MISS OF WILL

This subject requires the students to get the tons of knowledge and skills by their own, but a first semester student could not study all the required knowledge due of their "still growing maturation", this gets worse with the team's process because few members will learn the complete knowledge to deliver the project while the rest, the most, will just obtain a few learning.

- ## FEW TIME FOR TONS OF KNOWLEDGE

We understand that the objective of FIS is just to get the student to comprehend what software engineering means, and to decide if it's the good way for him, but the information about software engineering comes and goes quite fast, one doesn't get a chance to finish comprehend it while focusing on the project and other subjects, even in the didactic planning there are other big activities for each unit, activities we didn't make due of time, we agreed with "Ad astra" team: FIS should extend it's duration to be taken in two semesters, having FIS I and FIS II, because there is a lot of activities that requires team work, where scrum process and git skills can be perfectly practiced during two semesters, and the main project can also be lighter and better worked with more time.

- ## TEAM PROBLEMS

The students are almost left by their own to work in team full of inexpert people, the experts are supposed to rise during the subject, but this doesn't happen with every student, almost the same as the miss of will, some people will learn the required and some others won't finish comprehending, this generates problems in a team when the commitment of some members is much higher than some others, being this others the most, generating overwork, latements, fights and etc.

*This points describes the problems that come when unexperienced and undedicated students are set to take a subject that works with a lot of extra an personal study, this points can only be solutioned by better students, or well the subject program can change and be extended to cover better all posible information about FIS, a little more than just comprehending what software engineering is.*
