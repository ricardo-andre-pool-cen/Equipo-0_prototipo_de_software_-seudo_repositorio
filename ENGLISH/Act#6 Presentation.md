# VIDEO DRAFT

### First phase
The Documentation, Backlog, Todo, In progress, In review and Does sections were created in the activities_team#0_Second_Delivery section within the repository where the team leader assigned activities to the members, so that the members were independent and carried out the activities in their own time organization, said activities had a list of requirements included to be accepted as a correctly carried out activity, a general requirement in each activity was to use the English language in its entirety, to have the entire project in English and to add it to its corresponding folder, just as the team members kept a daily record of their activities in the project carried out during their day, each member added their activities and records in their personal branch, then they created their pull request, where a quality test was carried out by the leader, upon approving said pull request they proceeded to the implementation of said activity in the corresponding second delivery branch.
In the evolution of our project, we realized that we were creating a github 2 which is not a competent and clear goal, so we reorganized our ideas and now our project focuses on how to use github tools for teamwork or a project, normally there is very extensive and diverse information on how to use and learn git and github tools, but it does not explain how they work and the usefulness of these tools in teamwork.

### Second phase.
Therefore our goal is to teach users the functionality and impacts of using their individual knowledge in group work, but as our goal is big, for this second installment we will cover a very small part of our big goal, giving a small but well-defined and very useful part, showing our new visionary project, our small part focuses on git init and the search bar, this detailed information is in our repository, so do not hesitate to visit us.
Our requirements evolved to the point that they became new to adapt to our new project, our artifacts changed completely, such as user stories, the user landscape changed from high school and college students to university students, since currently there is enough evidence of the problems of university students in the use of git and github in a team collaboration, therefore our artifacts and user stories are focused on this new problem, resulting in our innovative project, where by teaching the use and operation of the git and github tools in teamwork, we will encourage students to learn how to use the tools and their impact on group work.
