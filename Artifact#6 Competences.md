## UNIT III: Evolution of Software Engineering

### We must have learned this specific competence from unit III

- *3: Chooses the software life cycle model at the beginning of a proyect, based on the characteristics of the development team, the customer and the problem*

### By doing this we applied the unit III competence

- Scrum process application: Backlog and sprints
- New process implementation: the sprint.

### Observations for unit III

For this second delivery, we chose a professional methodology to work our project, by using scrum, we organized the duration of our process in "sprints", and we learned to create a "backlog" so we can see all the work we could do and choose a couple of them only.

The idea of having "sprints", is to have short periods of work where we could not deliver a complete artifact for the second delivery, but we could have small artifacts of all the parts of the project, so we would visualize how is it going to be delivered at an early time.

we correctly chosed a life cycle for our project.
